# MoodMatch - The Social Mood Tracking Game

## Overview

MoodMatch is an open-source project that allows users to track and share their daily moods with friends and the community. The goal is to create a fun and engaging way for people to understand their emotional states and connect with others.

## Key Features

1. **Mood Tracking**: Users can log their moods throughout the day, categorizing them into different emotions like happy, sad, anxious, etc. They can also add notes and reflections.

2. **Mood Visualization**: Users can view their mood history in a calendar or graph format, allowing them to identify patterns and trends over time.

3. **Mood Sharing**: Users can choose to share their mood updates with friends, family, or the broader community. This fosters connection and empathy.

4. **Mood Challenges**: Users can participate in mood-themed challenges, such as keeping a positive mood streak or trying to experience a wider range of emotions.

5. **Mood Analytics**: The app provides insights and analysis on users' mood data, helping them better understand their emotional well-being.

## New Feature: Mood Matching and Recommendations

We're excited to announce a powerful new feature for MoodMatch - Mood Matching and Recommendations. This feature allows users to connect with others who are experiencing similar moods, fostering a sense of community and support.

- **Mood Matching**: Users can opt-in to have their mood updates matched with those of other users. When a match is found, the users will be notified and given the option to connect and chat.
- **Mood Recommendations**: Based on a user's mood history and preferences, the app will provide personalized recommendations for activities, content, or communities that may help improve or maintain their emotional well-being.
- **Mood-based Groups**: Users can join mood-based groups, where they can discuss their experiences, share coping strategies, and provide support to one another.

These new features aim to make MoodMatch an even more powerful tool for self-exploration, personal growth, and meaningful connections.
